# Marshall Micro Lab 🦠

Welcome to the **Marshall Micro Lab**

We are a research group at the **University of Florida** focused on developing and applying computational approaches to understand microbial populations, genomic diversity, and microbial epidemiology.

Our work combines **microbial genomics, metagenomics, bioinformatics, machine learning, and epidemiology** to develop practical tools for microbial characterization and surveillance.

## 🔬 Research Interests

* Microbial genomics and comparative genomics
* Genomic and molecular epidemiology
* Metagenomics and microbiome analysis
* Pathogen detection and characterization
* Bioinformatics tool and workflow development
* Machine learning for microbial genomics
* Spatial and environmental microbial surveillance

## 💻 Software

This organization hosts open-source software, workflows, and analysis tools developed by the Marshall Micro Lab.

Projects range from command-line bioinformatics tools to reproducible genomic analysis workflows and research software supporting microbial epidemiology.

## 🧬 Reproducible & Open Science

We aim to make our research software **open, documented, reproducible, and reusable**. Where possible, repositories include documentation, example datasets, reproducible workflows, and citation information.
